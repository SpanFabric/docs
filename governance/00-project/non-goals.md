# Non-goals
- Cloud-gaming VM where the whole game/OS executes remotely.
- Game/launcher plugins as the primary compatibility mechanism.
- DLL injection/hooking as the core design.
- Pretending a virtual device is a stock NVIDIA/AMD PCI device by reverse-engineering proprietary hardware interfaces for v1.
- Anti-cheat concealment or bypass.
- Guaranteed acceptance by every third party or guaranteed playable performance at every RTT.
- Multi-tenant marketplace/account/billing/GUI work before architecture proof.
